Model: separated continental foundations and monthly climate / surface biomes. Major continents are connected components covering at least 1% of the map; smaller fragments are measured separately. Gaps are shortest Euclidean coast-cell distances minus one cell, including horizontal wrapping. Open ocean counts water at least half the configured gap from any shore. Coverage is reconciled before archipelagos, lakes and erosion, so final land percentages may shift slightly. These cell-scale diagnostics measure the generated geometry, not projected physical distances. Enclosed depressions become water only when the hydrology budget supports them. Old worlds retain their stored geography.
